# Broker Explorer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Broker Explorer is developed by Magnus Egelberg.

## Data collection

The developer does not collect personal data, MQTT messages, broker credentials,
usage analytics or tracking identifiers through Broker Explorer. The app contains
no advertising or third-party analytics services.

## Your MQTT broker

The app connects to the MQTT broker you choose. Connection details and credentials
are sent to that broker as required to authenticate, subscribe and exchange
messages. Publishing and clearing retained messages sends the corresponding data
to the broker. The broker operator may store messages, connection logs and other
data under its own policies. Choose a broker you trust and use TLS when available.

## Data stored on your Mac

The app stores recent connection settings locally, including the broker address,
port, username and password, along with interface preferences. These settings use
macOS application preferences; broker passwords are not stored in Keychain.
Messages are held in memory while the app is running. The developer does not
receive these settings or messages. Avoid entering credentials on a shared or
untrusted Mac. Removing the app alone may not remove its saved preferences.

## Third-party services

Opening a project or support link uses your browser and the destination service's
privacy policy, including GitHub's policy. Do not include passwords, private MQTT
payloads or other confidential information in public support issues.

## Your choices

You choose which broker to connect to and can disconnect at any time. To request
instructions for removing locally stored preferences, or to ask a privacy
question, contact the developer through the project support page:
https://github.com/meg768/broker-explorer/issues

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes.
